# Grading assistant

Your sidebar shows **Grading** with **Dashboard** and **Courses**. You only see classes your instructors assigned to you.

## Start here

1. [All grading assistant actions](../actions/#grading-assistant) lists every step.
2. [Grading assistants](../features/grading-assistants) explains scope and limits.
3. [Grading and feedback](../features/grading-and-feedback) matches the grading screen you share with faculty.

## Typical task

1. Open **Courses**.
2. Open a **class**, then an **assignment**.
3. Open a **submission** and fill the rubric and feedback your instructor expects.

**Path (courses):** `/grading-assistant/courses`  
**Example URL:** `https://gradeforge.tech/grading-assistant/courses`
